The schedule almost every LM run uses: a short linear warmup so the early, badly-scaled updates stay small, a cosine decay through the bulk of training, then a constant floor.

$$\mathrm{lr}(t) = \begin{cases} \mathrm{lr}_{\max}\cdot t / T_w & t < T_w\\[2pt] \mathrm{lr}_{\min} + \tfrac{1}{2}\!\left(1 + \cos\!\left(\pi\,\tfrac{t - T_w}{T_c - T_w}\right)\right)\!\left(\mathrm{lr}_{\max} - \mathrm{lr}_{\min}\right) & T_w \le t \le T_c\\[2pt] \mathrm{lr}_{\min} & t > T_c \end{cases}$$

  • t — the current iteration
  • warmup_iters — $T_w$, where the peak lr_max is reached
  • cosine_iters — $T_c$, where the decay ends at lr_min

Return a plain float.

Hint

Three branches, no numpy needed beyond np.cos and np.pi. Check the endpoints: $t = 0$ gives $0$, $t = T_w$ gives exactly lr_max, and $t = T_c$ gives exactly lr_min.
