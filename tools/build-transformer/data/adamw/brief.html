One AdamW update for one parameter tensor. Adam keeps two running averages; AdamW's contribution is to pull the weight decay out of the gradient and apply it directly to the parameter, so it does not get rescaled by the second moment.

$$\begin{aligned} m &\leftarrow \beta_1 m + (1-\beta_1)\,g, &\quad \hat m &= m / (1-\beta_1^{\,t})\\ v &\leftarrow \beta_2 v + (1-\beta_2)\,g^2, &\quad \hat v &= v / (1-\beta_2^{\,t})\\ \theta &\leftarrow \theta \;-\; \mathrm{lr}\cdot\frac{\hat m}{\sqrt{\hat v} + \epsilon} \;-\; \mathrm{lr}\cdot\lambda\,\theta \end{aligned}$$

  • param, grad, m, v — arrays of the same shape
  • t — the step number, 1-based (bias correction divides by zero at $t=0$)
  • weight_decay — $\lambda$, applied to the parameter itself

Return the tuple (param, m, v), all updated. Do not modify the inputs in place.

Hint

Update the moments before the bias correction, and keep the corrected values out of the stored state — the m and v you return are the uncorrected ones. The decay term is $\mathrm{lr}\cdot\lambda\cdot\theta$, never $\lambda \cdot g$.
