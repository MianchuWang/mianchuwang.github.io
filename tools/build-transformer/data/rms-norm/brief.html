Llama, Qwen, DeepSeek and most recent models drop the mean subtraction and the bias from layer norm. What is left is a pure rescaling by the root-mean-square of the vector — cheaper, one fewer reduction, and empirically just as stable.

$$y_i = \gamma_i\,\frac{x_i}{\operatorname{RMS}(x)},\qquad \operatorname{RMS}(x) = \sqrt{\frac{1}{d}\sum_j x_j^2 + \epsilon}$$

  • x — (..., d); the RMS is taken over the last axis
  • gamma — (d,) learnable gain, no bias term
  • eps — inside the square root, default 1e-6
Hint

Do not centre the input: a row of all-ones must come back as all-ones, not zeros. reduce(x**2, "... d -> ... 1", "mean") is the einops way to keep the axis.
