The feed-forward half of a modern transformer block. Two projections go up, one gates the other elementwise, and a third comes back down. Naming follows Llama and CS336: W1 is the gate, W3 the up-projection, W2 the down-projection.

$$\operatorname{FFN}(x) = \bigl(\operatorname{SiLU}(x W_1^\top) \odot x W_3^\top\bigr)\, W_2^\top, \qquad \operatorname{SiLU}(z) = z\,\sigma(z)$$

  • x — (..., d_model)
  • W1, W3 — (d_ff, d_model)
  • W2 — (d_model, d_ff)

Return (..., d_model). There are no biases.

Hint

$\sigma(z) = 1 / (1 + e^{-z})$. SiLU is not ReLU — it stays smooth and slightly negative for small negative inputs. $\odot$ is a plain *.
